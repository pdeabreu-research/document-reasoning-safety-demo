# Synthetic Outputs (Demonstration Only)

These outputs correspond to the synthetic inputs in `synthetic_inputs.md`.  
They are **illustrative** and intended to demonstrate structure, calibration, and auditability—not factual claims.

## Example A Output

**Extracted Elements**
- Policy requirement: Identified (with indicated source section)
- Responsible role: Ambiguous (flagged for review)

**Evidence & Provenance**
- Source attribution: Present (section-level reference)
- Evidence sufficiency: Partial

**Uncertainty & Flags**
- Oversight responsibility is not explicitly defined
- Multiple plausible interpretations detected

**Confidence Calibration**
- Model confidence: Low–Moderate
- Confidence driver: Ambiguous role attribution

**Escalation Criteria**
- Requires human clarification prior to downstream use
- Escalate if outputs are considered for compliance or enforcement contexts

**Reviewer Notes**
- Request source clarification or supplemental context
- Avoid interpretation without explicit role definition

**Decision Boundaries**
- Outputs are advisory and must not be used for compliance enforcement
- Ambiguous role attribution requires explicit human clarification
- No downstream action should occur without reviewer confirmation

**Known Limitations**
- Cannot resolve oversight responsibility absent explicit source language
- Sensitive to vague or internally inconsistent policy phrasing
- Not robust to implicit governance assumptions

**Non-Goals**
- Determining compliance outcomes
- Assigning responsibility or liability
- Replacing organizational review processes



## Example B Output

**Extracted Claims**
- Primary objective statement (conditionally phrased)

**Evidence & Provenance**
- Source attribution: Present

**Consistency Checks**
- No direct contradictions detected
- Conditional language may be misread as definitive

**Uncertainty & Flags**
- Key claims rely on conditional phrasing
- Risk of overgeneralization detected

**Confidence Calibration**
- Model confidence: Low–Moderate
- Confidence driver: Conditional dependencies

**Escalation Criteria**
- Requires expert review prior to interpretation
- Escalate if summarized for decision-making contexts

**Reviewer Notes**
- Distinguish stated intent from implied outcomes
- Avoid summarization without contextual qualifiers

**Decision Boundaries**
- Outputs are interpretive aids, not research conclusions
- Conditional claims must not be summarized as definitive findings
- Expert review is required prior to dissemination or synthesis

**Known Limitations**
- Conditional language may be oversimplified in downstream summaries
- Does not assess methodological validity or experimental quality
- Sensitive to framing effects in research abstracts

**Non-Goals**
- Validating research findings
- Drawing causal inferences
- Substituting for domain expert judgment


## General Evaluation Lens

Across examples, outputs are evaluated for:
- Evidence grounding and attribution
- Calibration and uncertainty signaling
- Appropriateness of escalation triggers
- Clarity of human review requirements
