# Synthetic Inputs (Demonstration Only)

All examples in this directory are **synthetic** and created solely for
demonstration purposes. They do not correspond to real individuals,
organizations, events, or documents.

These inputs are intentionally underspecified to surface ambiguity,
uncertainty, and escalation boundaries in downstream reasoning.

See [synthetic_outputs.md](synthetic_outputs.md) for corresponding illustrative outputs.

---

## Example A Input — Governance / Decision Context (Synthetic)

A fictional policy excerpt containing:
- Broadly stated requirements
- Implicit oversight responsibilities
- Ambiguous role attribution
- Non-specific enforcement language

**Intent of this example**
- Stress-test role ambiguity
- Surface governance uncertainty
- Require human clarification prior to interpretation

**Notable characteristics**
- Vague responsibility assignment
- Multiple plausible readings
- Insufficiently explicit escalation language

---

## Example B Input — Analytical / Interpretive Context (Synthetic

A fictional research-style summary containing:
- Conditionally phrased objectives
- Dependent outcome statements
- Non-causal language that may be misread as definitive

**Intent of this example**
- Stress-test conditional reasoning
- Evaluate overgeneralization risk
- Require expert review before synthesis

**Notable characteristics**
- Heavy use of qualifiers
- Outcome dependencies
- Potential for misinterpretation if summarized naively
